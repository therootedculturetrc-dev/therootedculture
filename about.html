Our Story

Rooted in Culture.

The Rooted Culture™ started with a simple idea: your clothes should say something true about you — not something borrowed. We design for the in-between moments — chaos, calm, and everything that doesn't fit a label.

How it began

Built on a hostel desk, not a boardroom.

No big studio, no big budget — just a sketchbook, a few oversized blanks, and a stubborn belief that Indian streetwear could hold its own against anything from Tokyo or Seoul. Every early drop was tested on friends before it ever touched a rack.

What we make

Pieces that outlast the trend cycle.

Acid-washed tees, oversized silhouettes, ringer classics — each one designed to be worn hard and still hold its shape. We work in small batches so every print gets the attention it deserves, not the shortcuts mass production demands.

What We Stand For

🌱

Rooted, Not Copied

Every design starts from something real — a phrase, a memory, a mood — never a trend we're chasing.
